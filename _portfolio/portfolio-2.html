--- title: "Iron Accumulation, Brain Shrinkage, and Cognitive Aging" excerpt: "Four-wave longitudinal study examining associations among brain iron accumulation, regional atrophy, and fluid intelligence decline." collection: portfolio ---

This project investigates longitudinal associations among subcortical iron accumulation, brain shrinkage, and fluid intelligence decline using four waves of MRI and cognitive data spanning approximately eight years.

Research Questions

  • Does iron accumulation precede brain shrinkage?
  • Does brain shrinkage contribute to cognitive decline?
  • How are iron, brain structure, and cognition linked across aging?
