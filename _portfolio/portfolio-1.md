### Brain De-differentiation Across Aging

This project investigates whether age related changes in regional brain volumes become increasingly shared across the adult lifespan and how vascular, metabolic, and genetic factors modify these trajectories.

### [Iron Accumulation, Brain Shrinkage, and Cognitive Aging](https://ademgashi.com/portfolio/portfolio-2)

This project examines longitudinal associations among subcortical iron accumulation, brain shrinkage, and fluid intelligence decline using four waves of MRI and cognitive data.
