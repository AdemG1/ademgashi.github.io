Understanding change

A region is part of a network.

These descriptions highlight contributions, not exclusive functions. Memory, language, emotion, and movement emerge from systems working together.

Two people of the same age can have very different brains—and very different trajectories.

Aging summaries describe research patterns, not predictions for an individual. A smaller region does not automatically mean poorer performance.

What does “ROI” mean?

A region of interest: an area chosen for study. Some ROIs follow anatomical boundaries; others combine several areas. Definitions differ across atlases and research protocols.

Why use four different views?

A lateral view hides medial and deep structures. The medial view reveals the inner cortical surface; the coronal view opens the hemispheres; the temporal cutaway exposes the memory system. No single view shows everything.

Does brain aging mean dementia?

No. Dementia is not an inevitable consequence of aging. Occasional forgetfulness is different from persistent difficulties that interfere with everyday independence. NIA: memory and aging ↗

Do these drawings show exact atlas boundaries?

No. These are original anatomical teaching illustrations with approximate ROI boundaries. They are not subject-specific MRI segmentations, a standardized atlas, or a tool for locating a lesion. Shape, size, and folding vary between people.
