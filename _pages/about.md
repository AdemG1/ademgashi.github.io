## Welcome

I am a PhD candidate in Neuroscience at the University of Geneva investigating how vascular and metabolic health shape trajectories of brain and cognitive aging across the adult lifespan.

My research integrates longitudinal neuroimaging, cognitive assessments, and biological markers, including blood based and genetic indicators, to understand how vascular risk, lifestyle, and environmental factors influence individual differences in brain structure and cognitive function over time.

## Current Research

### Biological Modifiers of Brain and Cognitive Aging

My doctoral research examines how vascular, metabolic, genetic, lifestyle, and environmental factors contribute to individual differences in age related changes in brain structure and cognitive performance.

Current projects focus on:

* Brain iron accumulation and cognitive aging
* Regional brain atrophy across adulthood
* Vascular and metabolic risk factors
* Longitudinal neuroimaging and cognition
* Structural equation modeling and dynamic longitudinal methods

---

## Featured Question

> Why do some individuals maintain brain and cognitive health well into older age while others experience accelerated decline?

My research seeks to answer this question by integrating neuroimaging, cognitive, biological, and health related data collected over many years.
